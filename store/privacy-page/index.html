Privacy Policy

AIUB Slide Downloader (Chrome extension) · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

AIUB Slide Downloader saves your own course files from the AIUB student portal and your AIUB Microsoft Teams classes into a folder you choose. Everything happens in your browser. No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What the extension handles

  • The names of your courses and course files on portal.aiub.edu and on your AIUB Teams (SharePoint) class sites.
  • A list of the files it has already saved (file name, folder and a size/date marker), so nothing is downloaded twice.
  • Your first name, only to show "Welcome, <name>" in the panel.
  • The Teams class sites you opened, so they can be found again faster.

Where it is kept

Only on your own computer: in the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local), in the browser storage of portal.aiub.edu (to remember which folder you chose), and in the AIUB folder you chose. The extension reads and writes only inside that folder.

Folder access

You choose the folder yourself in Chrome's own folder window, and Chrome asks for your permission. The permission is given to the portal page (portal.aiub.edu), where the extension's panel runs. Program files (such as .exe, .bat, .msi) are never saved. You can take the permission back at any time: on the portal, click the icon left of the address bar → Site settings → File editing.

Network requests

The extension connects only to portal.aiub.edu and aiubedu60714.sharepoint.com, using your existing sign-in, to list and download your files. It never reads or stores your password.

Reporting a problem

Only if you press "Report problem", a page opens with technical details (extension version, page address path, number of course links found). No names, course titles or personal data are included, and nothing is sent unless you submit it yourself.

Sharing or selling data

Your data is never sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and never used for any purpose other than downloading your course files.

Removing your data

Remove the extension, or clear its data in chrome://extensions. Downloaded files stay in your folder until you delete them.
